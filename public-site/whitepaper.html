01 · PREMISE

The world stays put. The ruler changes.

Human measurement systems often accumulate unrelated bases, conversion factors and special-purpose units. A8 asks whether many of those jobs can instead share one binary architecture.

The design rule is not that Nature is binary. Binary and octal are engineered representations chosen because exact halving, doubling and machine-state grouping are useful. Geometry, physics, trigonometry and π are unchanged.

02 · A8/PERFECT

Throw away machinery, not calculability.

A8/PERFECT is the working simplification criterion: retain the minimum independent machinery required to preserve complete derivation, forward calculation and reverse calculation.

REMOVE A CONCEPT → RECALCULATE → IF THE RELATIONSHIP REMAINS REVERSIBLE, THE REMOVED CONCEPT WAS REDUNDANT

This principle is why A8 NAV no longer requires a conversion to A8/41. They are the same physical length. It is also why a separate orbital-distance ruler is unnecessary: Spaceship Earth's motion can already be expressed directly on the ordinary A8 length ladder.

Simplification stops when another removal would prevent a quantity from being recovered, checked or calculated in reverse.

08 · SPEED

Push the rung. Not the number.

A8 does not need a different speed unit for every class of motion. Choose one elapsed-time denominator, calculate the distance traveled during it, then select the binary length rung that places the coefficient nearest one.

SPEED = DISTANCE / ELAPSED A8 TIME

For the standard transport view, the denominator is one A8 HOUR. Let D be the distance traveled in A8 NAV during that hour.

n = 41 + round(log₂(D))
coefficient = D / 2^(n − 41)

The physical motion does not change when the rung changes. Only its most convenient binary address changes.

13 · ENGINEERING DISCIPLINE

Native first. Translation second.

A8 separates exact architecture from comparison values.

EXACT powers of two
EXACT register states and counts
EXACT NAV = A8/41
APPROXIMATE legacy comparison decimals

Public approximate decimal values are normally displayed to no more than four decimal places. That is a presentation rule, not a numerical limitation. Internal calculations may retain whatever precision is required.

Independent recoverability

A physical proof is stronger when the result can be reconstructed from event identities and raw counted oscillator edges rather than from an ordinary timestamp supplied by the host operating system.

Application separation

Presentation software may visualize, translate and teach the recovered state. It must not silently become the authority from which the state supposedly originated.

Reversibility

Whenever practical, a relationship should survive calculation in both directions:

distance / time = speed
speed × time = distance
distance / speed = time

This is the engineering boundary behind A8/PERFECT: simplification is desirable only while calculability survives.
