Auspicious 8

An experimental measurement architecture built from naturally recoverable phenomena outward, with binary/eightfold representation designed for the machines that must ultimately count, store and transmit the measurements.

Public Research Note · Version 1.3 · 2026 · COSIGO Labs

1 · The question

Historical measurement systems are extraordinarily useful, but many of their rulers were optimized for administrative practice, hand calculation, inherited geometry and the instruments of earlier eras. A8 asks a narrower engineering question: if a system were being designed today, could natural recurrence and binary machinery be connected more directly?

Governing distinction: the natural phenomenon is not the register, and the register is not Nature. A register is a finite engineered ruler placed on a physical phenomenon.

2 · Physical time and independent recovery

The present A8 time research uses the Jovian moons Io, Europa and Ganymede as three parallel observational reference channels. Each channel is intended to be independently recoverable from repeated observations against one arbitrary monotonic local pulse counter. Their 1:2:4 structure permits a common normalized comparison while preserving disagreement as evidence rather than averaging it away. Callisto is retained as a slower witness and research channel.

The research path deliberately separates the defining physical observation from conventional timestamps. Real-observation replay work therefore reduces telescope observations to a signed Jupiter-relative displacement plus a raw pulse count.

3 · The Earth-day clock

For the human daily clock, one Earth day is represented as one A8 day divided into 32 A8 hours, each A8 hour into 64 A8 minutes, and each A8 minute into 64 A8 seconds. This produces exactly 131,072 states, or 2¹⁷ states, across the day.

decimal comfort: 32 × 64 × 64 = 131,072 = 2¹⁷
native octal: 40₈ × 100₈ × 100₈ = 400000₈ states

The native engineering form is a seventeen-bit day-phase register. Octal provides a compact human-readable representation because each octal digit corresponds exactly to three binary bits. Decimal may remain as a comfort display during adoption without becoming the native definition. Bare legacy labels are avoided: these are A8 HOUR, A8 MINUTE and A8 SECOND subdivisions, not conventional hour/minute/second units.

4 · Angle

A8 represents a full turn with 512 states, exactly 2⁹. This does not alter circle geometry, π or trigonometry. It changes only the ruler placed around the circle.

full turn = 512 states = 1000₈ rollover → 000₈

Quarter, half and eighth turns fall on exact binary boundaries. The three-octal-digit range 000₈–777₈ maps directly to the nine-bit range 000000000₂–111111111₂.

5 · Why this public page is simpler

The public instrument intentionally hides recovery epochs, calibration controls, replay modes, diagnostic tolerances and fault injection used by the engineering laboratory. Its clock samples the visitor’s local wall clock once when the page loads or when the visitor explicitly resynchronizes it, then free-runs from the browser’s monotonic timer. That local wall-clock sample is a presentation alignment only; it is not claimed as the physical definition of A8 time.

The Jovian display on the public page is likewise a visualization of the architecture, not a live telescope feed or recovery authority. Its motion is deliberately accelerated and the page displays the selected plant-step rate plus an approximate ×N natural Ganymede-rate reference so the animation cannot be mistaken for natural orbital speed.

6 · Work demonstrated so far

Engineering work has covered arbitrary-oscillator recovery, parallel Io/Europa/Ganymede channels, holdover behavior, equal-duration 8:4:2 qualification, real-observation replay contracts, relative-pixel telescope reduction, simulated CCD/counter instruments, noise, periodic missing observations and irregular observatory gaps. Some tests intentionally end in rejection or holdover; those outcomes are evidence about observability rather than failures to be hidden.

7 · Future progression

Possible future work includes real telescope/counter data, distributed observing sites, longer-baseline qualification, the relationship between terrestrial observing windows and Jovian recurrence, calendar design, a native A8 calculator and exploration of other physical measurement domains. These are research directions, not claims that every domain will reduce neatly to the same structure.
