Lab Method

1. Observe

Start with a reproducible phenomenon: recurrence, ratio, symmetry, lattice, phase, spectrum, growth, motion, field behavior, or another measurable structure.

2. Separate layers

Do not confuse Nature with the register, the register with the unit, or the unit with a legacy conversion. State clearly what is physical and what is representation.

3. Reproduce

Publish enough method that another person can independently obtain the same result—or show where it fails.

Current Research Domains

DEPENDENCY LOCKEDACTIVE

Music, Audio & Scale

Current clue: the Jovian 1:2:4 relationship already produces a natural ratio structure that can be rendered audibly.

  • Io : Europa : Ganymede carrier structure = 1:2:4.
  • Callisto may function as a slower envelope/check rather than primary authority.
  • East/west reversals can become clicks or phase markers.
  • Simultaneous channels can be heard as a composite resonance.
Boundary: legacy Hz values are only an audio transducer layer for speakers and browsers. They do not define A8 time. Final tones should derive from the native A8 clock.

Next experiment: derive a fully native A8 pitch/interval ladder from recovered A8 phase and compare perceived consonance against ordinary equal temperament without forcing a result.

OPEN RESEARCH

Biology, Chemistry & Natural Scaling

Current clue: living and chemical systems repeatedly exhibit doubling, halving, pairing, recursive growth, symmetry and cyclic ratios.

  • Cellular doubling and halving.
  • Mitosis / meiosis structure.
  • DNA replication and complementary pairing.
  • Molecular and atomic ratios.
  • Biochemical cycles.
  • 2→4→8 structures, fractal/recursive growth, symmetry and scaling laws.
Rule: observe first. Do not retrofit biological or chemical data to make it “look A8.”

Next experiment: select one reproducible biological doubling system and one molecular ratio system, express both in native ratio form, and test whether A8 representation simplifies or merely relabels them.

ACTIVE

Quartz Convergence

Current clue: one naturally abundant crystalline material keeps appearing across several measurement domains.

  • Space: ordered lattice recurrence.
  • Distance witness: α-quartz a- and c-axis recurrences measured against the sealed Earth-defined A8 length ladder.
  • Time carrier: mechanical resonance / quartz oscillators.
  • Piezoelectric coupling: mechanical ↔ electrical response.
  • Sensing: strain, force, pressure and frequency response.
  • Optics: prism behavior, birefringence, optical activity and refraction.
  • 3-D structure: axial c recurrence and transverse a-plane recurrence.

Next experiment: treat a and c as independent spatial witnesses, express them on the sealed A8 ladder, characterize their ratio and environmental dependence, and test optical/piezoelectric observables against the same crystal state.

Longitude: 0 → 512 Latitude: North 0 → Equator 128 → South 256 Hour-meridian spacing: 16 A8 angular units

Electricity, Magnetism & Fields

DEFERRED

Atomic & Subatomic Measurement

Current clue: once time, angle, length, temperature and mass are native, atomic phenomena can be measured without first translating through legacy units.

No promised answer. Do not assume atoms “must” reveal an 8-pattern. Measure them and see what the A8 ruler reveals.

Next experiment: after base-unit coherence, re-express selected spectral wavelengths, transition frequencies, atomic dimensions and dimensionless constants in A8 units and look for simplification or new ratios.

Why This Page Exists

No one person has to finish every A8 domain. The project becomes stronger if each unfinished clue is documented well enough that a physicist, musician, biologist, chemist, geologist, engineer, programmer, navigator, or student can pick up one thread and run a real experiment.

Discover → document → reproduce → challenge → refine → hand off

Failure is useful. A domain that does not simplify under A8 should be recorded just as carefully as one that does.
