AUSPICIOUS 8 · MATHEMATICAL CHECK

Earth Rotation Closure

A self-contained exercise for checking the relationship among the native A8 angular ruler, civil clock, Earth-surface navigation distance, mean rotational motion and binary length ladder. No legacy unit is required for the structural proof.

Given

1 full turn = 2⁹ = 512 native A8 angular units
1 Earth day = 32 × 64 × 64 = 2¹⁷ A8 SECOND
1 A8 MINUTE = 64 = 2⁶ A8 SECOND
1 A8 NAV := CE,mean / 2¹⁵

CE,mean is the mean-Earth circumference reference used by the A8 NAV construction.

Exercise A · Angle → Clock

How much rotational time corresponds to one native A8 angular unit?

2¹⁷ / 2⁹ = 2⁸ = 256 A8 SECOND
256 / 64 = 4 A8 MINUTE

1 A8 map degree = 4 A8 minutes of Earth rotation

“A8 map degree” here means one native 1/512-turn angular unit, not one legacy 1/360 degree.

Exercise B · Distance → Speed → Time

One native angular-unit arc along the mean-Earth circumference is:

CE,mean / 2⁹

But:

A8 NAV = CE,mean / 2¹⁵

Therefore:

(C / 2⁹) / (C / 2¹⁵) = 2⁶ = 64

1 A8 angular-unit arc = 64 A8 NAV

Mean rotational motion travels:

C / 2¹⁷ = (C / 2¹⁵) / 2² = ¼ A8 NAV per A8 SECOND

Time required to travel 64 NAV:

64 NAV ÷ (¼ NAV / A8 SECOND) = 256 A8 SECOND = 4 A8 MINUTE

Cross-check

The angle-and-clock route and the distance-and-speed route were calculated differently. They meet at the same exact result:

ANGLE + CLOCK → 4 A8 MINUTE DISTANCE + ROTATIONAL SPEED → 4 A8 MINUTE

Equivalently:

1 A8 angular-unit arc = 64 A8 NAV mean rotation = ¼ A8 NAV / A8 SECOND 64 ÷ ¼ = 256 A8 SECOND = 4 A8 MINUTE

Exercise C · Binary ladder check

Under the current operational A8/31 realization, A8 NAV occupies this position on the binary ladder:

1 A8 NAV ≈ 1.037786960558 A8/41

The distance traveled by mean Earth rotation in one A8 SECOND is exactly one quarter of NAV. One quarter is 2⁻², so the ladder position moves exactly two rungs:

¼ × 1.037786960558 A8/41 = 1.037786960558 A8/39

The coefficient stays the same. The rung changes by exactly two.

Reverse it

A check should work in both directions. Starting with rotational travel:

1.037786960558 A8/39 × 2¹⁷ A8 SECOND = 1.037786960558 A8/56 = CE,mean

Or recover NAV:

1.037786960558 A8/39 × 4 = 1.037786960558 A8/41 = 1 A8 NAV

Or recover one map-angle arc:

1.037786960558 A8/39 × 256 = 1.037786960558 A8/47 = CE,mean / 512

What this confirms

This is an internal mathematical consistency check. It confirms that the A8 angular ruler, day structure, minute structure, NAV definition, mean rotational-speed expression and binary ladder agree with one another.

It is not an independent remeasurement of Earth's circumference: the circumference is an input to the NAV and rotational-distance relationships being cross-checked.
