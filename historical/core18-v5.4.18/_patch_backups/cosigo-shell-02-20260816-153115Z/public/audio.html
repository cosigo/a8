Why the A8 ladder is naturally octave-friendly

A musical octave is a physical 2:1 frequency ratio. A8 does not create that acoustic relationship; it represents it cleanly because binary doubling and halving are already native operations.

The 2:1 octave ratio is the physical/acoustic fact. The unusually clean sequence 256 → 512 → 1024 is the A8 representation of that fact. This is “octave-friendly by structure,” not a claim that Nature itself uses a human numeral system.
