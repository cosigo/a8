What this establishes

The raw-pulse foundation can physically exist as an analog oscillator producing electrical transitions counted by ATmega328P hardware Timer1 peripherals.

Observation identity is separate from raw-pulse evidence. When the physical source is stopped, identities do not cause the hardware count or Pi bridge representation of that count to advance.

What this does not establish

These experiments do not claim Arduino or Pi software is incapable of error or deception, nor that the LM555 is a final astronomical oscillator. Source, firmware, raw files and hashes remain inspectable.
