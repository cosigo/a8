WHAT 512 − 1 = 511 MEANS

Start with Terra at one orientation relative to the stars and Sol. Terra then rotates, but Terra is not sitting still while it rotates: the entire planet is simultaneously advancing around Sol. Terra Ship Slip is the A8 expression of that rotation / orbit mismatch.

1 · FULL AXIAL TURN 512 A8 · 1000₈ recurrence One complete axial rotation relative to the stellar witness. The stored A8 angular states run 000₈ → 777₈; 1000₈ is the return to the starting direction.
2 · ORBITAL ADVANCE DURING THAT TURN 1 A8 During that same complete axial rotation, the recovered Core20 relationship shows Terra advancing by 1 A8 angle around Sol.
3 · SUN CLOSURE PER AXIAL TURN 511 A8 · 777₈ Because Terra moved forward while rotating, one stellar axial turn does not produce one complete Sun return. The Sun-relative closure gained during that axial rotation is: 512 − 1 = 511 A8.
4 · COMPLETE SUN RETURN 512 / 511 axial rotations A complete Sun-relative return requires the full 512 A8 closure. At 511 A8 of closure per axial rotation, the recovered relationship is therefore 512/511 axial rotations per Sun return.
