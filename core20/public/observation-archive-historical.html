Separation rule

Archive inspection may contain conventional source timestamps, published periods and other source metadata. Those values remain source evidence and do not become defining Core20 timing authority merely because they are present in an archive.

A source file crosses the observation boundary only after its physical columns are understood and a deterministic reduction is verified. A three-column mutual-event light curve is not called an apparent x-position stream unless a documented physical reduction creates one.
