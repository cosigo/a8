BCSEEK contributor call

Write for BCSEEK

BCSEEK is seeking reputable article writers, Bitcoin node operators, technical reviewers, infrastructure researchers, and serious independent voices to publish dispatches about Bitcoin Core bootstrap burden, BTC Monolith, ordinary node survival, AssumeUTXO, UTXO snapshots, bandwidth cost, hardware inflation, and decentralization risk.

The goal is not hype. The goal is serious public reporting before ordinary Bitcoin node operation becomes so heavy that only large infrastructure operators remain comfortable.

Contributor focus areas

  • Bitcoin Core initial block download and sync burden
  • Pruned node operation and ordinary-user limitations
  • AssumeUTXO, loadtxoutset, dumptxoutset, and UTXO snapshots
  • Storage, bandwidth, hardware, heat, and inflation pressure on node operators
  • Independent verification and practical decentralization risk
  • Reports from actual node operators around the world
  • Mirror infrastructure, checksum processes, and bad-file rejection testing
  • Plain-English explanations for non-technical bitcoin holders

Wanted voices

Technical writers Node operators Bitcoin educators Security reviewers Infrastructure analysts Storage/bandwidth experts Independent researchers

Submission standards

BCSEEK wants clear, sourced, original writing. Strong opinions are acceptable. Unsupported claims, recycled marketing copy, plagiarism, spam, and paid promotional articles are not.

  • Use your real name, pen name, or organization name clearly.
  • State your relevant experience or operating background.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest.
  • Provide sources, logs, measurements, or operator evidence when possible.
  • Keep claims about Bitcoin Core, node operation, and infrastructure technically honest.

What BCSEEK will not publish

BCSEEK is not a pump channel, token listing outlet, anonymous smear site, or financial advice publication.

  • No investment promises or price predictions.
  • No paid promotional articles disguised as research.
  • No copied articles without permission.
  • No malware links, wallet-drain links, or suspicious downloads.
  • No unsupported accusations presented as fact.

Suggested article formats

  • Operator dispatch: what hardware you run, what failed, what worked, and what it costs.
  • Technical explainer: plain-English explanation of one Bitcoin Core burden or feature.
  • Measurement report: sync time, disk use, bandwidth use, CPU heat, RAM, and failure points.
  • Opinion essay: a clear argument about decentralization risk and ordinary-node survival.
  • Review note: technical review of BTC Monolith assumptions, methods, or public claims.

Contributor updates

BCSEEK will use this page to post contributor guidance, topic requests, editorial updates, and calls for specific measurements or technical review.

First request: node operators willing to document real-world Bitcoin Core sync burden, especially on ordinary laptops, small desktops, home servers, and modest VPS systems.
