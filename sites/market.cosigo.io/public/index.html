CIGO-WBNB pool status
The CIGO-WBNB liquidity pool is currently dormant to protect market stability while liquidity and demand remain limited. This is a deliberate temporary status, not an abandonment of the market. If customer participation and genuine demand later make a second trading pair useful and sustainable, the pool may be adequately funded and returned to service. Until then, CIGO/BSC-USD (USDT) remains the primary active market.
