ABOUT THIS TIME

The 32-hour face shows the whole A8 day in one revolution. The 16-position face makes two revolutions: PASS 1 covers hours 00–15, and PASS 2 covers hours 16–31. Noon is A8 16:00, the start of PASS 2. Midnight is 32:00 / 00:00, the new day.
