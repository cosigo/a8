GANYMEDE · FUN FACTS

Largest moon in the Solar System

Ganymede is about 4,304.53 A8 NAV across — larger in diameter than Mercury.

A8 NAV native · legacy diameter witness · NASA
A moon with its own magnetic field

It is the only moon known to generate its own magnetic field, producing auroral activity around its poles.

External astronomy context · NASA / Galileo
An ocean under the ice

Observations support a global subsurface saltwater ocean, possibly holding more water than all of Earth’s surface oceans.

External astronomy context · NASA / Hubble
Galileo saw it in 1610

Galileo Galilei identified Ganymede with Jupiter’s other large moons in January 1610.

External astronomy context · NASA
Locked toward Jupiter

Ganymede is tidally locked, so the same hemisphere continually faces Jupiter.

External astronomy context · NASA
The 1 : 2 : 4 family

Ganymede, Europa and Io participate in the familiar 1 : 2 : 4 orbital resonance. In A8, each body keeps its own natural recurrence.
