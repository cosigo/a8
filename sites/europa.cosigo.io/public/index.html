EUROPA · FUN FACTS

An ice-covered ocean world

Strong evidence indicates a global saltwater ocean beneath Europa’s icy shell.

External astronomy context · NASA
About 2,536.9 A8 NAV across

Europa is about 90% the diameter of Earth’s Moon.

A8 NAV native · legacy diameter witness · NASA
Locked toward Jupiter

Europa is tidally locked, so the same hemisphere continually faces Jupiter.

External astronomy context · NASA
Galileo saw it in 1610

Europa was discovered in January 1610 during the first telescopic observations of Jupiter’s large moons.

External astronomy context · NASA
A young, fractured surface

Europa’s bright water-ice surface is crossed by long cracks, ridges and bands and has relatively few large impact craters.

External astronomy context · NASA / Galileo
The 1 : 2 : 4 family

Ganymede, Europa and Io participate in the familiar 1 : 2 : 4 orbital resonance. In A8, each body keeps its own natural recurrence.
