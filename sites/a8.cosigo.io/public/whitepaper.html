A8 Native Mathematics · Geometry, Matter and Motion

These calculations express physical measurements as native A8 quantities. Conventional measurements may be used to check a result but do not replace the established A8 reference rulers. Rung indices are ordinary numeric coordinates. Each whole rung changes a positive magnitude by a factor of two.

A8L · Length

rL = 31 + log2(L / L31)
L = L31 × 2^(rL - 31)

L31 is the established physical A8L 31 length reference. L and L31 must use the same physical dimension and scale. Positive lengths occupy the binary ladder. Zero length is a separate physical state.

A8A · Area

A31 = L31 × L31
rA = rL1 + rL2 - 31
A = A31 × 2^(rA - 31)

A8A 31 represents the square of A8L 31. For rectangular area, the two A8L coordinates combine through addition with the reference offset. The physical lengths multiply; their rung numbers do not.

A8V · Volume

V31 = L31 × L31 × L31
rV = rL1 + rL2 + rL3 - 62
V = V31 × 2^(rV - 31)

A8V 31 represents the cube of A8L 31. Other positive volumes follow the same binary ladder. The formula shown combines three perpendicular lengths for a rectangular solid.

A8M · Mass

M31 = mass of reference water occupying A8V 31
Reference conditions: A8T 31 and A8P 31
rM = 31 + log2(M / M31)
M = M31 × 2^(rM - 31)

The reference mass is defined by the existing A8 water-volume construction under the specified temperature and pressure conditions. The water reference must be reproducible before numerical external conversions are certified.

A8D · Density

Mwater = reference-water mass for the same volume
rD = log2(Mobject / Mwater)
Mobject / Mwater = 2^rD
Reference-water density: A8D 0

Density compares mass against an equal volume of reference water. A8D 0 represents the reference density, not zero physical density. Positive rung coordinates indicate greater density; negative coordinates indicate lower density. The comparison requires the stated water reference conditions.

rL = rS + log2(tH)

A8 Public Display Precision

COSIGO public instruments display numerical measurements using a maximum of four digits after the decimal point. Unnecessary trailing zeros are omitted. Values are rounded for presentation, never truncated or rounded before calculation.

This display rule does not modify native A8 definitions, reference anchors, exact binary relationships, physical calibrations, astronomical calculations, or internal computational precision. Researchers can calculate additional precision from the published equations and reference data.

Exact integers, fractions, octal states, timestamps, source identifiers, machine-readable data, and engineering evidence retain their required precision. Very small nonzero measurements must not be represented misleadingly as zero; use an appropriate native rung, scientific notation, or an explicit display threshold.
