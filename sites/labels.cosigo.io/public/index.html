Important notice for users, reviewers, and labelers

CIGO is presented by COSIGO as a controlled-risk, administratively managed market-access token with protective features intended to limit bot pressure, whale concentration, and liquidity abuse during early market formation. Those controls may raise centralization concerns and deserve open review, but COSIGO does not regard them as evidence of fraud, exploit behavior, or malicious token design.

COSIGO has disputed third-party wallet and scanner labels that impair normal CIGO access without clearly identifying a specific abusive function, confirmed misconduct, scanner rule, data source, or remediation path. Holders should review public disclosures, understand that CIGO is not an unmanaged meme asset, and approach trades carefully while adverse labels remain in place.

Wallet caution: MetaMask-related warning flows have been inconsistent in CIGO’s documented experience. Verify wallet behavior before relying on it, and consider Rabby Wallet for normal CIGO access.

MetaMask’s conduct in this matter was not a neutral act of user protection.
