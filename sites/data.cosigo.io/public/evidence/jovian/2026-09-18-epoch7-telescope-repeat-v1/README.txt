PROCESS
The feature image was produced from the two Sep-18 Jupiter telescope
recordings by aligning frames on Jupiter, rejecting poor/smeared
frames, preserving moon points that persist independently across
both recordings, and using the sharpest frame subset for Jupiter
disk detail.

2,133 aligned telescope frames contributed to the field stack.
The sharpest 534 frames contributed to Jupiter disk detail.

The image was converted to monochrome and given restrained
contrast/sharpening for public viewing.

No generated astronomical objects or synthetic moon positions
were added.

The A8 REF is a representative reference-frame stamp for the
stacked observation and is not a claim that all contributing
photons were recorded at one instant.
